========================================================================
HOW TO REPLACE WITH YOUR ORIGINAL RESUME PDF
========================================================================

Your portfolio is configured to serve your resume directly from:
   public/resume.pdf

To replace it with your real original resume:
1. Copy your original PDF resume file.
2. Rename it to "resume.pdf".
3. Paste / overwrite it inside the "public" folder of this project:
   -> /public/resume.pdf

That's it! When you push to GitHub or build your project:
- Visitors clicking "Download PDF" or "Open PDF" will immediately see your official original resume.
========================================================================
